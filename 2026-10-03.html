🔥 Top 3 (must read)

AI is changing developer work. Here are three skills to strengthen.

GitHub Blog · github.blog
GitHub says the developer career ladder is being rewritten by AI. The three skills to build are directing AI agents, reviewing their output with a critical eye, and keeping technical judgment at the center of the workflow. Why it matters: this is a ready-made frame for 1:1s and growth plans when your team asks "what should I get better at now?"

Anthropic invests $100M to train 10k engineers

Anthropic · anthropic.com
Anthropic is launching the Claude Frontier Academy, a program to train 10,000 engineers on building with Claude. It is a large, structured push to teach agentic development rather than one more model release. Why it matters: a free or vendor-backed training track could be a cheap way to upskill a team on Claude Code and agent patterns.

KubeCon NA 2026: OpenTofu Day

CNCF Blog · cncf.io
OpenTofu Day on November 9 covers everything provisioned before and around a cluster: cloud accounts, networking, managed services, and the clusters themselves. Most KubeCon content assumes the cluster already exists, so this fills the gap. Why it matters: if your Terraform repos are weighing an OpenTofu move, the talk list will show where the community is heading.

💡 App ideas & indie builds

Audionaut – open-source cross-platform multitrack audio editor

github.com
(HN discussion, 132 points) A free multitrack audio editor that runs on every desktop OS. The user problem: Audacity feels dated and pro DAWs are heavy and expensive, so podcasters and hobby musicians lack a simple middle option. Inspiration: a focused, open-source take on a tool people already pay for can get traction fast, and the HN thread shows what features users ask for first.
