🔥 Top 3 (must read)

tokeneff: An Open-Source LLM Cost Meter That Runs Locally

dev.to
Most LLM dashboards only show the bill after you spent the money. tokeneff runs on your machine and shows which model and which request cost what, while a coding agent is running. Why it matters: if your team runs Claude Code or Copilot agents all day, this gives you a way to see the cost per task before the invoice arrives.

Quoting Paul Ford

simonwillison.net
Simon Willison quotes a New York Times opinion piece by Paul Ford on AI and developer jobs. Ford says AI can write very good software, but real cutting-edge work still needs humans who think and work together. Why it matters: a short, clear argument you can use when your team asks "what is our job now?".

EP225: Why Does Git Revert Cause Conflicts?

blog.bytebytego.com
ByteByteGo explains why git revert looks simple but often throws a conflict. It walks through what revert really does to the history. Why it matters: reverts are the fastest way to roll back a bad deploy. Knowing where they break saves time during an incident.
